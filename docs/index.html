Know what you’re seeing

Transparent data, without a black box.

01   Recorded before the day

Each model run is saved when this app actually receives it. Model time and collection time are shown separately.

02   Review after the fact

Once the NWS daily climate report arrives, we compare it with the earliest forecast we recorded for that day.

03   Research, not a trade

Daily reports are not automatically prediction-market settlement results. No market prices or trading signals are included.
